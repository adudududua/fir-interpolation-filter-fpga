Same-DSP comparison
-------------------
At 2 DSP, CIC saves 54 LUT and 41 FF versus all-2x. BRAM/MMCM/DSP are equal.
At 3 DSP, CIC saves 75 LUT and 41 FF versus all-2x. BRAM/MMCM/DSP are equal.
CIC 3-DSP saves 35 LUT versus CIC 2-DSP at the cost of one DSP48E1.
CIC 6-DSP remains the absolute minimum-LUT point, while CIC 2-DSP is the
absolute minimum-DSP point. CIC 3-DSP is the balanced low-DSP/low-LUT point.

RTL/MATLAB acceptance
---------------------
Both CIC low-DSP profiles passed the national-finals RTL regression 9/9.
The low-DSP CIC and the original CIC were cycle-by-cycle identical for
continuous input, randomized stalls, and reset in the middle of a burst.
The formal full-chain impulse and random-PCM comparisons were 0 LSB at the
4x/8x/128x nodes. Eight internal-state reset scenarios and ten reset-free
dynamic mode switches passed. The published RTL impulse response passed all
six 44.1/48-kHz x 4x/8x/128x MATLAB acceptance cases.

Strategy scans
--------------
Synthesis: five combinations per profile. The valid best setting was
AreaOptimized_high + flatten_hierarchy=rebuilt + resource_sharing=on.
flatten_hierarchy=full was rejected because it removes hierarchy referenced by
the board clock XDC.

Default is selected because it ties the best LUT result and is the least
specialized reproducible implementation setting.
