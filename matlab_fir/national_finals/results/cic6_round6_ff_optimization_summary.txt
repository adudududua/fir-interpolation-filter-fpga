Selected change
---------------
Move the final hidden CIC integrator state from the asynchronous-reset
control/output process into the synchronous-reset integrator-state process.
The externally visible y_out/y_out_valid and transaction control retain
asynchronous reset. Routed DSP48E1 properties show that the final state is
absorbed by internal A/B input registers (AREG=1, BREG=1), not PREG.
